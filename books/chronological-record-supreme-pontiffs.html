CHRONOLOGICAL RECORD
OF THE
SUPREME PONTIFFS

$65

A name gains meaning when you understand its place in history. Chronological Record of the Supreme Pontiffs traces the Roman Catholic Church’s papal succession from Saint Peter to Pope Leo XIV. Follow the sequence across generations and place individual pontiffs within the longer story of the Church. Consider what succession reveals about continuity, responsibility, and the passage of time. Use the chronological structure to orient your reading and return to the record when a name sparks a new question. Deepen your historical understanding one pontificate at a time.
