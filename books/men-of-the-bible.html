MEN OF THE BIBLE

$99

God calls people to Himself because He wants them to know Him, receive His lavish love, and share His purpose. Men of the Bible explores how He makes Himself known through chosen men across different times, generations, and eras. Follow His patient pursuit of a people who belong to Him. Examine how these men respond to His call and how their decisions shape the next chapter of His work for future generations. Discover what their stories reveal about God’s character, His determination to reveal Himself, and His desire to draw people into a lasting relationship with Him.
