AMERICAN PRESIDENTS

$55

The presidency reveals both the power and the limits of leadership. American Presidents explores the people who have held the nation’s highest office since 1789 and invites you to consider the legacies they leave. Look beyond familiar names and ask how character, judgment, public expectations, and national circumstances shape leadership. Compare the promise of an office with the responsibilities of its occupants. Read with curiosity, examine presidential influence with care, and develop a more thoughtful understanding of American history.
