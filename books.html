READ WITH PURPOSE. LIVE WHAT YOU LEARN.

God is a writer. He inscribed His commandments on stone and gave us His Word through those He called to write. As life changes and new questions arise, our need for relevant books continues: books that preserve truth, deepen faith, make difficult subjects clear, and equip us to act with understanding. Read thoughtfully, return often, and put what you learn into practice.
